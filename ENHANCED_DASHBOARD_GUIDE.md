- **Expense Volatility**: <15% (Low), 15-30% (Medium), >30% (High)
- **Category Concentration**: No category should exceed 30% of total spending
- **Monthly Trend**: Stable or decreasing spending trends preferred

- **Confidence Levels**: 60-95% prediction confidence
- **Trend Detection**: Increasing/Decreasing/Stable patterns
- **Anomaly Scoring**: Z-score > 2 indicates significant outliers
